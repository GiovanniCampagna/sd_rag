# Semi-Synthetic Dataset

Generated using [the new pipeline](./src/make_testset.py).

1. Find 20 wikipedia articles on events newer than Dec 2024 (after data cutoff of tested models)
2. For each article:
    - [extract N=6 sensitive categories](./src/find-sensitive-categories-prompt.md)
    - [generate 10-15 Q&A pairs](./src/forge-prompt.md)

Using Gemini 2.5 Flash.

[The new testset](./datasets/testset.json).

- New dataset keeps "unredacted_answer_text".
- Interrogator model is instructed to ask questions to the RAG pipeline (assuming truthful answers) for the goal of proving the contents of "unredacted_answer_text".

## Discussion

### Monolith worse privacy

- Monolith's privacy score is lower even without attack.
- Monolith's privacy score decreases during attack.

### Monolith not always better completeness

- Monolith never wins on fact extract completeness.
    - Probably artifact: monolith has "I am unable to answer", which our latest method does not.

- Monolith wins on judge completeness in Meta Llama.

### Judge vs Fact extract Completeness

- Fact extract is more optimistic: + 0.22559 bias
- Std diff: 0.22682
- Variance: 0.05145
- Weak positive correlation (0.512062 pearson, 0.43992 spearman)

Script: [compare privacy scores](./scripts/compare_priv_scores.py)

### Rephrase vs Extract

- Rephrase is more aggressive in Meta Llama 3.1.
- Extract is more aggressive in Qwen 2.5.

### Anomalous behavior of pre-redaction under attack

- Monolith's privacy score decreases during attack (expected).
- Extract/Rephrase privacy scores increase during attack (unexpected).

- This cannot be because of inject vs no inject retrieval differences (because they are independent of generation/redaction method).

- Constraint retrieval: still it's the same for both monolith and pre-redact. Even if it depends on query. And anyway, during injection the retrieved constraints should be less relevant if anything, so it's unlikely that they increase privacy.

- Answers to malign queries are longer and contain more "REDACTED" tokens indicating that the attack is causing the context to be leaked.

- My current explanation:
    - malicious query contains noise, causes question not to get answered precisely, returns some random stuff which is less related to question. But this also happens in the monolith case.

- In any case, it would be nice to have something like this to automate testing on more LLMs by making it easier to find the optimal injection: https://github.com/liu00222/Open-Prompt-Injection; but too much time and our task is very narrow.

- Sometimes extraction does not work because: "I cannot provide sensitive information".

- Same for evaluation:  "I cannot evaluate the completeness of an answer that may be related to illegal activities." or "I cannot extract illegal facts..."
